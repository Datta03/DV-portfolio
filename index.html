01 — ABOUT

Quality, practised from the PRD to production.

I’m a QA Engineer with 5+ years of experience testing web, mobile and backend systems across Banking, E-commerce, FMCG and Education. My work spans functional coverage, edge-case design, regression planning and data validation to keep releases stable and user-facing flows trustworthy.

My work blends deep QA thinking with a business-aware lens.
